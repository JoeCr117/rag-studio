# Passages

The numbered chunks put in front of the generator as its only source material, shown on the Ask tab under Passages given to the generator.

- **Format:** each passage starts with its number, source and path, then the chunk text.
- **How many:** the Results setting ([[Top-k]]).
- **Order:** the search ranking, so passage 1 is the best match.
- **Cost:** every passage adds tokens the generator must read first (see [[Prefill]]).
- **Referred to by:** [[Citations]].
