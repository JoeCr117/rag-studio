# Chunking

Cutting each file into chunks: pieces of text small enough to embed and to hand to the generator, but large enough to make sense on their own.

- [[Chunk]] — one piece of a file, the unit that is stored and searched
- [[Chunk size]] — the smallest and largest a chunk may be
- [[Chunk overlap]] — text repeated across a cut so ideas stay whole
- [[Tokens]] — the pieces of text a model reads and writes
- [[Tokenizer]] — the part of a model that turns text into tokens
