# Vector norm

The length of a vector: the square root of the sum of its squared numbers.

- **Why it is shown:** a quick health check. The embedding server usually scales vectors to length 1, so a norm near 1.000 is expected.
- **Why length rarely matters:** [[Cosine similarity]] compares only the direction of two vectors, not their length.
- **In the Browse tab:** the norm pill on each chunk.
