# BM25

Best Matching 25, a classic keyword-ranking formula: a chunk scores higher the more often it contains the question's words, the rarer those words are overall and the shorter the chunk.

- **Rare words count most:** through inverse document frequency (IDF), a word found in few chunks is worth more than one found everywhere.
- **Diminishing returns:** the tenth repeat of a word adds far less than the first.
- **Sparse vector:** Qdrant stores BM25 as a sparse vector, with one entry per word that appears in the chunk and nothing for the rest.
- **Built in:** computed by [[Qdrant]] itself, inside its [[Docker image]], so nothing is downloaded.
- **Used by:** [[Hybrid search]] in Qdrant databases.
